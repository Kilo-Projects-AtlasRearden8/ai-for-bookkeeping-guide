# Read Me First

## Education only — not tax advice

This guide is educational material published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**. It is
**not** tax, accounting, legal, or financial advice. It does not create a
client relationship with any accountant, enrolled agent, CPA, or attorney.

Tax rules change, and they depend on your location, your business structure,
and your specific situation. Before you file anything or make a tax decision,
check IRS.gov (or your state or provincial tax agency) and talk to a qualified
tax professional — a CPA, enrolled agent (EA), or tax preparer.

AI can help you organize information and draft records, but AI can also get
things wrong. Never file a tax return based solely on what an AI tells you.
Always verify numbers against your bank statements, receipts, and a human
professional when it matters.

## Who this guide is for

You run a small business or side hustle — maybe freelance writing, direct
sales, reselling on eBay or Amazon, KDP publishing, or a service business — and
you want to use AI to keep your books organized without becoming an accountant.
You do not need any bookkeeping or AI experience to use this guide.

## How to use this guide

Read the chapters in order, or jump to the one you need right now:

| Chapter | What it covers |
|---------|----------------|
| 1. AI bookkeeping basics | What AI can (and can't) do for your books, picking tools, staying safe |
| 2. Tracking income | Recording every dollar that comes in |
| 3. Categorizing expenses | Sorting spending into tax-friendly categories |
| 4. Receipts | Capturing, storing, and keeping receipts |
| 5. Quarterly tax prep | Estimated taxes, deadlines, and a prep checklist |
| 6. When to hire a pro | Signs it's time to bring in a human professional |

Each chapter ends with plain-English AI prompts you can copy and paste, plus a
printable worksheet in `docs/worksheets/` (and matching pages in `site/`).

## A note on privacy

Your financial data is sensitive. In this guide we recommend **local-first**
habits: keep your master records on your own computer, and avoid pasting real
bank account numbers, Social Security numbers, or customer lists into public AI
tools. See Chapter 1 for a simple privacy checklist.
