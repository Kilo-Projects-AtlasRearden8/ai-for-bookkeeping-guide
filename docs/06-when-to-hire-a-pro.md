# 6. When to Hire a Pro

AI can organize your books, but a human tax professional is responsible for
your return. Know when it is time to bring one in.

## Signs you should hire a CPA, enrolled agent, or tax preparer

- You formed an LLC, partnership, or S corporation and are not sure which
  forms to file
- You have employees or contractors (payroll taxes have strict rules)
- You sell in multiple states and may owe sales tax in states where you do not
  live
- You received an IRS notice or letter
- You are unsure whether a big purchase is deductible
- Your books are a year (or more) behind
- You want help calculating estimated tax
- You just want peace of mind before you file

## What a pro can do that AI cannot

- Sign and file your tax return
- Represent you before the IRS if there is an audit or question
- Give advice tailored to your business, state, and income
- Keep up with tax law changes

## What to bring your pro

Bring your income log, expense tracker, receipts, mileage log, and bank
statements — all the things this guide helps you keep organized. The cleaner
your records, the less your pro charges and the fewer mistakes there are.

## Finding a pro

Look for a CPA (Certified Public Accountant), an enrolled agent (EA — federally
licensed tax practitioner), or a reputable tax preparer. Ask:

- Do you work with small businesses like mine?
- What do you charge, and what is included?
- Will you review my AI-organized records?

## AI is not a replacement

AI can draft records and suggest categories, but it cannot sign your return or
stand behind you in front of the IRS. Use AI to keep clean books all year, then
hand those clean books to a qualified professional at tax time.
