# 3. Categorizing Expenses

Expenses are what your business spends to operate. Categories are labels you
put on each expense so your tax preparer (or tax software) can figure out which
are deductible. AI is good at suggesting categories, but you make the final
call.

## Common small-business expense categories

These are common U.S. Schedule C-style categories. Your exact list may differ
— ask your tax professional which ones apply to you.

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Advertising | Business cards, website ads, promoted posts |
| Office expenses | Postage, printer ink, stationery |
| Supplies | Items you use up quickly (packing materials, pens) |
| Rent | Office or studio space you rent |
| Utilities | Electricity, internet, phone for business use |
| Meals | Business meals (usually 50% deductible — ask your pro) |
| Travel | Hotels, airfare for business trips |
| Vehicle | Business miles, parking, gas (keep a mileage log) |
| Professional services | CPA, lawyer, tax preparer fees |
| Software & subscriptions | Accounting software, AI tools, website hosting |
| Shipping & delivery | Postage to send products to customers |
| Cost of goods sold | Items you buy to resell (books, inventory) |
| Home office | Portion of home expenses if you qualify (ask your pro) |

"Ordinary and necessary" is the standard phrase: an expense is ordinary if it is
common in your line of work, and necessary if it is helpful and appropriate.
Ask your tax professional before you deduct anything unusual.

## How to categorize with AI

Paste a list of expenses and let AI suggest categories, then review every
suggestion yourself. AI does not know your business as well as you do.

Prompt — Categorize expenses:

```
I am keeping my own small-business bookkeeping records. Below is a list of
expenses. For each one, suggest the most likely category from this list:
Advertising, Office expenses, Supplies, Rent, Utilities, Meals, Travel,
Vehicle, Professional services, Software & subscriptions, Shipping,
Cost of goods sold, Home office, Other.

Mark anything unclear with [?]. Do not give tax advice — I will verify with my
tax professional.

Expenses:
...
```

## Review AI's suggestions

AI might label a lunch with a friend as "Meals" when it was personal. It might
call a new laptop "Supplies" when your tax pro prefers it tracked as equipment.
Read every row and change anything that does not fit your business.

## Keep receipts

A category label is not proof of spending. Keep the receipt, invoice, or bank
record for every expense (Chapter 4).

## Mileage tip

If you drive for business, track miles in a simple log: date, start and end
addresses, purpose, and miles. The IRS lets you deduct business miles at a
standard rate that changes each year — check IRS.gov for the current rate and
ask your tax professional how to use it.
