Worksheet: Income Log

Print one per month. Record every payment your business receives. Keep this with your bank statements.

Date Customer / source Description Amount Payment method Deposited to Invoice # Notes
Total income this month: $______________
  • Record the gross amount the customer paid, not just what hit your bank account.
  • Record payment platform fees as expenses, not as reduced income.
  • Keep business income in a separate business account when you can.
