AI for Small-Business Bookkeeping

A plain-English beginner manual on using AI for small-business bookkeeping: categorizing expenses, tracking income, receipts, quarterly tax prep, and knowing when to hire a pro. Includes printable worksheets.

Written and published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026) — the LLC behind Dylan Asdale's book The Massive AI Manual and hub site dylanasdale.github.io. AI explained like a friend would: plain English, step by step, never condescending.

How to use this site

Read the chapters in order, or jump to the one you need. Every chapter has copy-and-paste AI prompts in plain English. The worksheets are designed to print — open a worksheet page and press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac).

The site is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No installation, no server, no accounts. Open site/index.html in any browser.
